## Hi there, I'm Om 👋

I'm an undergraduate student and an aspiring engineer working at the intersection of **Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and Robotics**.

### Currently

* Research Intern at the **Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems (MPI-IS), Tübingen**
* Working on **3D parametric models, image-to-3D reconstruction, and data generation pipelines**
* Team Manager at **Project MANAS**, working on autonomous systems and robotics

### Research Interests

* **3D Computer Vision & Reconstruction**
* **Deep Learning Systems**
* **Embodied / Physical AI**
